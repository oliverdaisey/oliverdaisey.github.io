A meeting discussing new developments in representation theory of algebras, combinatorics, algebraic geometry, polytopal geometry, cluster algebras and some aspects of theoretical physics. After a 5-day school centred around 4 mini-courses on some new directions of research, the event resumed with a 5-day conference that included a broader range of subjects and speakers, as well as a poster session.
